Privacy policy

Real Life Japanese. Last updated November 6, 2026.

The app has no accounts and no server of its own. It does not collect personal data, and it does not track you.

Your recordings

When you practice a reply, the app records your voice to play it back and, on iOS 26 or later, to check your reply using speech recognition on your device. Recordings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time in Settings. They are also removed if you delete the app.

Your progress

Your progress, stamps, review items, and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them. iOS may include them in your own device backup, depending on your settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, payment details, or Apple account. The app asks Apple whether your purchase is active. Apple may report sales totals to us that don't identify you.

Notifications

Review reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
